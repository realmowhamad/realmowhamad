# 💫 About Me:

Oh hello there! 😁

I'm **Mohammad Mirhoseini**, a Front-End Developer with over 5 years of experience — including 3 years at [Saman Electronic Payment (SEP.ir)](https://www.sep.ir/), where I built real-time dashboards and internal tools to support millions of payment operations.

Since moving to the UK, I’ve been working as a freelance **Full-Stack Developer**, building intuitive and modern web applications for a range of clients. My focus is always on usability, maintainability, and performance — whether I’m creating a landing page, a dashboard, or a custom booking system.

✨ **Why I love what I do:**  
I’m passionate about bringing ideas to life through code. I enjoy working with React, Next.js, Python, and Django to solve real problems with simple, elegant solutions.

🚀 **Projects I’m proud of:**
- **[bita-beauty.co.uk](https://bita-beauty.co.uk)** – A beauty salon website with full admin panel for services, gallery, and bookings.
- **[maryamabbasi.co.uk](https://maryamabbasi.co.uk)** – Personal brand blog with embedded video, ad management, and a CMS-style dashboard.
- **[persianriverside.co.uk](https://persianriverside.co.uk)** – Restaurant website with menu management and reservations.
- **[riversideautomobiles.co.uk](https://riversideautomobiles.co.uk)** – Dynamic car listings and admin-controlled inventory.
- **[mistatech.co.uk](https://mistatech.co.uk)** – My own digital agency site.

💻 **What I use daily:**  
I speak JavaScript and Python more than my native tongue. I regularly work with:
- Front-End: React, Next.js, Tailwind, Redux
- Back-End: Django, Node.js, Express
- CMS & UI Tools: WordPress, Figma
- Deployment: Vercel, Netlify, Heroku, DigitalOcean

✏️ Outside of development, I enjoy contributing to open-source, learning new frameworks, and automating workflows to reduce repetition and improve efficiency.

👥 **Let’s connect:**  
I love working with others to solve real-world problems and turn vision into a digital reality. Collaboration, clarity, and clean code are my top priorities.

📫 Drop me a message anytime — I’d be happy to connect!
